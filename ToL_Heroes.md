# Tyrants of Lothal — Hero Builds

**Party:** CT-1701 · Loku Kanoloa · Jarrod Kelvin · Murne Rin

---

## Murne Rin — False Orders Support

**Role:** Extra attacks and battlefield control. Leave big guns and XP for the damage dealers.

**Imperial Commander note:** Skip **Double Agent** — the stun half is weak under IC rules. Push is nice but not worth 3 XP here. Race **Waylay** instead.

### Build order

ToL starts at **2 XP**. Bank it toward Waylay.

1. **Hold → Waylay** (4) — False Orders → bonus hero attack (and ally if in range). Buy as soon as you hit 4 XP.
2. **Rebel Propaganda** (2) — strain discard / team utility
3. **Professional Aide** (2) — surge help for allies/heroes
4. **Company of Heroes** (1) — IC2 rules (not tabletop −4 cost): if you brought an optional unique ally, **deplete** at mission start to **defeat one figure** from the Imperial optional deployment (figure cost ≤4)

Skip or delay Aide / CoH if XP is tighter elsewhere. **Double Agent** only if you specifically want the push later.

### Play notes

- Stay within 3 of a good False Orders target; set up so CT or Loku can take the Waylay attack.
- Cheap weapon is fine — her action economy is False Orders, not shooting.
- If high-cost uniques make False Orders rare, pivot toward **Lead from the Front** later.

## CT-1701 — Barrage Spray (Wild Fire)

**Role:** Ranged spray / volume fire (Barrage, multi-attacks). Loku is the heavy precision shot — CT softens packs and dumps attacks. Murne covers support, so skip Concentrate Fire / Squad Tactics.

**Imperial Commander note:** Skip early **Pin Them Down**. Stun is weak under IC rules (nulls Move MPs, then clears). Weakened is still okay later; not worth the opener.

Damage spine without PTD: Reload, Strafing Run, Weakness Identified, Wild Fire. Guides still call **Wild Fire** the Masterstroke-equivalent 4XP.

### Build order

Spend starting **2 XP** on mobility — every Barrage/Wild Fire attack pays for itself in movement.

1. **Strafing Run** (2) — movement points off ranged attacks; workhorse with multi-attack
2. **Reload** (1) — power-token cap to 3; rest → 2 damage tokens
3. **Hold → Wild Fire** (4) — Barrage identity; routinely 4 attacks/round with a decent gun
4. **Weakness Identified** (3) — strip a defense die (kills the Barrage white die)
5. **Covering Fire** (1) — block token for Loku (or yourself) after an attack

**Pin Them Down** only as spare XP late, and only for the Weakened half.

### Play notes

- E-11 is on; Charged Ammo Pack helps token economy. Prefer non-exhaust damage mods later (e.g. Plasma Cell) over Tac Display — you attack too many times per round.
- Barrage is strain-heavy; rest + Reload keeps tokens flowing.

## Loku Kanoloa — Classic Sniper

**Role:** Heavy precision shot. Mark priority targets with recon tokens; delete them (and feed +1 dmg / Combat Spotter LoS to CT and Waylay).

Plots and Plans preferred package: Combat Spotter, Overwatch, Coordinated Attack, Mon Cala Special Forces (11 XP).

### Build order

Starting **2 XP**: take Spotter, bank the rest toward Overwatch.

1. **Combat Spotter** (1) — ignore figures for LoS to reconned targets (you + allies); bank 1
2. **Overwatch** (2) — interrupt attack when a marked figure moves/attacks
3. **Hold → Mon Cala Special Forces** (4) — strain recover / +accuracy / +evade; priority over CA
4. **Coordinated Attack** (4) — extra attack die vs a reconned target (any attack, not just yours)

Skip Study of Enemies / Scout’s Guidance unless XP is loose after the above.

### Play notes

- Recon tokens are a hard limit of 2 — re-place by pulling an existing token if the marked figure runs.
- Credits: upgrade past the All-Weather Rifle when you can (A280 / DXR-6 often cited).
- Stay far back; you’re fragile — CT’s Covering Fire (and positioning) help keep heat off you.

## Jarrod Kelvin — Melee Assault

**Role:** Frontline melee / assault tank. Jax is Supporting Fire and a strain sink, not the main damage plan (no **X-8 Upgrade** in ToL).

Plots and Plans Jarrod-focused package: Balanced Approach, Scout’s Loadout, Explosive Reflexes, Leaping Slash.

### Build order

Spend starting **2 XP** on team utility — Scout’s Loadout helps CT/Loku from mission 1.

1. **Scout’s Loadout** (2) — −1 Evade on an attack you or Jax can see; small evade-token on deploy
2. **Balanced Approach** (1) — recover / power tokens when you mix action types
3. **Hold → Explosive Reflexes** (3) — +1 Endurance (more Jax calls); mess with attacker dice
4. **Hold → Leaping Slash** (4) — strain to move + melee attack (not locked to Vibro-Claws)

Skip the Jax package (Mechanical Mastery / Mutual Progression / Slicer’s Upgrades) — it wants X-8.

### Play notes

- Buy a real melee weapon early (Vibroblade → Polearm if it shows). Vibro-Claws are for Parry and early game only.
- Keep Jax adjacent to enemies for Supporting Fire pierce; expect him to die often — that’s fine.
- You’re the body in front so Loku can stay long-range.
